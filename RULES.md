# The app's rules (plain words)

Every rule the app follows, in plain words. When something changes, this file changes in the same go. Rules that get replaced come out.

## Getting in
- Only your login gets in. Nobody can sign up.
- The app remembers you, so you sign in once.

## Home screen
- Top: the app's name and a greeting (Good morning / afternoon / evening). The gear opens Settings.
- The box at the top saves an idea into the tile picked in the row under it (it starts on the first tile). Paste a link and it's saved with the idea.
- Insights card: your views for the last 7 days, your followers, and a small line of your last 14 days. Tap it for the full Insights.
- Sponsors card: how many brand deals are waiting on you. Tap it for the Sponsors page.
- To-dos card: add a to-do, and the next few (urgent first, then today's). "Calendar ›" opens Life.
- Tiles: each one opens its page. They start as Category 1, 2 and 3.

## Life (smart to-dos)
- Type a to-do with a day or time ("dentist Friday 3pm", "pay rent on the 1st", "call mom tomorrow") and it goes on that day, due by that time. The words for the day and time come out of the to-do's name.
- Every to-do sorts itself into a card: Money & credit, Appointments, Returns & shipping, Moving, Car, Tech & apps, Content & work, Home & chores, Personal, Shopping & errands, Family, Pets, Health, or Other.
- Move a to-do to another card once (tap it → Group) and similar ones go there from then on. You can make your own cards, rename them, merge two, and pin one to the front (📌).
- The cards stack like a deck: swipe the top card to see the next one. Urgent to-dos (🚨) glow red at the top of their card, and cards with urgent ones come first.
- The calendar shows this week, or the full month: a purple dot per to-do, a red one when it's urgent. Tap a day to see its rundown (to-dos by time, then the rest) and add a to-do for that day.
- To-dos with no day get one from the planner: spread over the next two weeks, a few per day, similar errands on the same day, urgent ones today (after 5 PM it starts tomorrow). A day you pick yourself never moves. "No day" keeps a to-do off the calendar.
- A to-do left from an earlier day shows on today until it's done.

## Sponsors
- Twice a day your Claude reads your Gmail for brand deals (it only reads; it never sends, deletes or changes anything) and puts each deal on a card: the brand, the offer, what they want, the timing, and a short summary.
- Every new message gets a reply drafted in your style (your rates, tone and sign-off, saved in SPONSOR-STYLE.md in your private repo). Tell Claude to change your style or rates anytime.
- Each new email gets a scam check. Asking you to pay anything, crypto, gift cards, logins, or "claim" links = ⚠️ "Looks like a scam", and no reply is drafted.
- On a card: edit the reply, tap Open in Gmail to send it yourself (or copy it), then tap "I replied". Skip hides a deal until they write again.
- Waiting on you = new or answered by them. Replied and Skipped ones sit under "Show".

## Tiles
- Tap **Edit** above the tiles, then tap a tile to rename it, pick an emoji (or type any emoji) and a color, move it left or right, or delete it.
- **New tile** adds one (up to 12).
- Deleting a tile moves its ideas to the first tile left (you tap twice to be sure).

## Ideas
- A tile's page shows its ideas, newest first. Done ideas hide under "Done".
- Tap an idea to change its title and details, add or remove links, move it to another tile, mark it done, or delete it (tap twice).
- After deleting something, or finishing an idea, a little message offers **Undo** for a few seconds.

## Insights
- A copy of Instagram's own Insights screens, with your real numbers.
- Your numbers come from Instagram with your own Instagram key: new posts and their numbers every 15 minutes, the charts (views per day, followers, accounts reached, audience) every 6 hours.
- The line under the top bar says when it last updated, or what went wrong.
- Double-tap the back arrow to go full screen like Instagram; tap it once to bring the top bar back. The X goes home.
- A post can be linked to one of your done ideas ("Link this post to an idea").

## Settings
- Change the app's name. See whether Instagram is connected and when it last updated. Sign out.

## Updates
- When a new version is ready, the app reloads itself the next time you open it.
