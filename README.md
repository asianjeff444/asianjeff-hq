# Your starter app

A starter iPhone home-screen app: blank tiles you make your own, to-dos, idea pages, and an Instagram Insights replica with your real numbers.

**How to use this:**

1. Start a new chat with Claude.
2. Attach this whole zip file.
3. Say: **"Read START-HERE.md and help me set this up."**

Claude walks you through everything, one step at a time (a free GitHub account, a free Supabase account, your Instagram key, and adding the app to your home screen). After that, just tell Claude what you want to change.
